# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Shoppers discover, follow, and buy independent brands. Brand operators use Studio to onboard and manage their catalog and storefront. Yard staff use the admin tools to operate the marketplace.

## Product Purpose

Yard is a global, discovery-driven marketplace for independent brands. It helps shoppers find relevant pieces and helps brands reach customers through a managed storefront and commerce workflow.

## Brand Personality

Curious, independent, confident.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic marketplace presentation, pastel palettes, soft gradients, and decorative glow. Do not let promotional framing obscure product discovery or operational tasks.

## Design Principles

- Put discovery and useful product information ahead of promotional framing.
- Keep shopper, merchant, and admin workflows distinct and task-focused.
- Make commerce state, price, and availability clear and trustworthy.
- Preserve Yard's bold editorial identity without sacrificing usability.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve semantic structure, keyboard operation and visible focus, respect reduced-motion preferences, and support reflow at narrow mobile widths.
