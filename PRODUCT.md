# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Shoppers discover, follow, and buy from independent brands and trusted stores. Brand and store operators use Studio to onboard and manage their catalog and storefront. Yard staff use the admin tools to operate the marketplace.

## Product Purpose

Yard is a global, discovery-driven marketplace for independent brands and trusted stores, including retailers and resellers of genuine products. Each seller is responsible for selling genuine products. It helps shoppers find relevant pieces and helps sellers reach customers through a managed storefront and commerce workflow.

## Brand Personality

Curious, independent, confident.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic marketplace presentation, pastel palettes, soft gradients, and decorative glow. Do not let promotional framing obscure product discovery or operational tasks.

## Design Principles

- Put discovery and useful product information ahead of promotional framing.
- Keep shopper, merchant, and admin workflows distinct and task-focused.
- Make commerce state, price, and availability clear and trustworthy.
- Preserve Yard's bold editorial identity without sacrificing usability.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve semantic structure, keyboard operation and visible focus, respect reduced-motion preferences, and support reflow at narrow mobile widths.
